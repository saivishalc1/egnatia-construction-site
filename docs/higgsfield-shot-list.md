## 1. The style line (append to EVERY prompt)

This is what makes the set look like one shoot instead of random AI images:

> Cinematic, shot on ARRI Alexa 35 with a 35mm lens, blue-hour and warm tungsten light, deep warm shadows, muted palette of brownstone, walnut, brass and charcoal, soft film grain, shallow depth of field, photorealistic, no faces, no text, no logos, no watermark.

Tips:
- Generate 3–4 variations of each shot and send me the best 1–2. Consistency beats novelty.
- Keep the same model for all stills and the same model for all videos.
- For video, pick a **slow** camera-motion preset (dolly in / push in). Fast moves look cheap and don't loop well.
- No cuts inside a clip.
